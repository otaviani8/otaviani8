### What I work on

- **AI agents in production:** tool use, structured output, prompt caching, guardrails, and evals with scripted and LLM-simulated users.
- **Lead-to-revenue systems:** capture from Lead Ads, WhatsApp and the web, AI qualification and routing, CRM sync, offline conversions back to Meta.
- **Paid acquisition:** Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn.
  - Account architecture for parallel clients.
  - Systematic creative testing.
  - Optimization to qualified-lead and sale events.
- **GTM automation:**
  - n8n, Make, Zapier, GoHighLevel and HubSpot.
  - Enrichment with Clay and Apollo.
  - Custom code where no-code stops.
- **Creative at scale:** AI video and image pipelines, automated editing, reporting dashboards.
